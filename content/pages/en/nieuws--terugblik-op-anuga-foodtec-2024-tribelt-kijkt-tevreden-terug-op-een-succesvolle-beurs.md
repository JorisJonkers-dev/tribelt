## Four days in Cologne

A team of Tribelt specialists exhibited at Anuga FoodTec 2024 in Cologne, Germany. Over four days they introduced visitors to metal conveyor belts and their uses, covering almost the full range, from the many eyelink belt versions to Tribelt's own [Tri-Flex](/en/conveyor-belts/tri-flex) belt.

## Why this fair matters

Many Tribelt belts are used in food production, so Anuga FoodTec is a recurring and important event for the company. The fair is a chance to present innovations and to meet both existing customers and new contacts.

Tribelt looks back on a successful fair and thanks every visitor for their time and interest. Anyone who missed the stand can get in touch with one of Tribelt's sales staff via the [contact page](/en/contact).
