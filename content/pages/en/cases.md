## Projects with Tribelt customers

The cases below show how Tribelt works with its customers: thinking along about the process, solving technical problems and delivering belts that make a production line more efficient. Each story describes a real project in which Tribelt's conveyor belt know-how made a measurable difference.

## Glapor: sustainability built into construction

German manufacturer Glapor makes cellular (foam) glass insulation from recycled glass. Since 2019 it has worked with Tribelt on metal belts that carry the glass through a high-temperature furnace, aiming for longer belt life and less downtime. [Read the Glapor case](/en/cases/glapor).
