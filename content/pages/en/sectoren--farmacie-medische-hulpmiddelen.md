## Hygiene, reliability and accuracy

Pharmaceutical and medical device production sets the bar very high for hygiene and precision. Glass ampoules, vials and medical devices are often sterilised or disinfected in dedicated lines, and the belts in those lines must resist heat, steam and chemicals while carrying products cleanly and steadily. Tribelt, a manufacturer of metal conveyor belts in Haaksbergen, the Netherlands, supplies belts for these processes.

Material choice and design are decisive. Belts and components have to survive repeated sterilisation cycles and still stay accurate for years. Cleanability, easy maintenance and minimal contamination risk are central, so products reach patients with maximum certainty.

## Processes Tribelt supports

- **Raw materials and ingredients:** careful, hygienic handling of powders, liquids and small components between production steps. Width, length and material (stainless steel or corrosion-resistant alloys) are customised to meet hygiene and GMP standards, and configuration, speed and cleaning options are set with the customer.
- **Assembly and processing:** assembling medical devices or packing products demands exact positioning. Belts can have positioning systems, adjustable guides and sensors; modular belts, tilting units and special coatings protect sensitive or sterile parts.
- **Packing and labelling:** belts support automated packing and labelling with steady throughput and precise positioning. Speed synchronisation, gentle transfers and simple cleaning improve safety and product quality.
