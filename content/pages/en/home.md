## Tribelt in one paragraph

Tribelt B.V. is a Dutch manufacturer of metal conveyor belts and the drives that run them, based at Metaalstraat 9 in Haaksbergen, the Netherlands. Founded in 2002 and part of the Swedish Lesjöfors Group since 2019, Tribelt builds belts for bakeries, potato, vegetable, meat and dairy processors, pharmaceutical plants, recyclers and machine builders. Every belt is made to order in Tribelt's own factory.

Tribelt does more than weave wire. Its engineers help choose the belt type and material, carry out strength calculations and work out how the belt fits into the customer's machine. If required, Tribelt installs the belt on site or supports the fitters, so the result matches the production environment it has to work in.

## Belt types

- [Eyelink belts](/en/conveyor-belts/eye-link-belts): stable wire-eye belts for ovens, freezers, dryers and washers
- [Open spiral belts](/en/conveyor-belts/balanced-weave-belts) (balanced weave): open woven spirals that let heat and air through, up to about 1,150 °C
- [Closed spiral belts](/en/conveyor-belts/compound-balanced-belts) (compound balanced): a dense surface for small products and heat treatment
- [Tri-Flex](/en/conveyor-belts/tri-flex): a stainless steel belt that runs straight or through the curves of a spiral freezer or cooler
- [Drive elements](/en/conveyor-belts/drive-elements): sprockets, tube drums and cage drums machined in-house
- [Other belts](/en/conveyor-belts/other-belts): chain, ladder, plate link, honeycomb and woven baking belts

All belts are listed on the [metal conveyor belts](/en/metal-conveyor-belts) overview.

## Sectors Tribelt serves

Tribelt's belts keep production lines moving in a wide range of industries:

- **[Bread and pastry](/en/sectors/bread-pastry):** belts that touch food directly, with a declaration of conformity with European food-contact rules available on request.
- **[Potatoes, fruit and vegetables](/en/sectors/potato-fruit-and-vegetables):** washing, blanching, frying and freezing lines that demand hygiene and wear resistance.
- **[Meat, fish and dairy](/en/sectors/meat-fish-and-dairy):** easy-to-clean stainless steel belts that carry heavy loads.
- **[Meat substitutes](/en/sectors/meat-substitutes):** hygienic, scalable lines for plant-based protein products.
- **[Bakery and oven builders](/en/sectors/bakery-and-oven-builders)** and **[production line builders](/en/sectors/production-lines-process-installations):** heat-resistant belts built to the machine builder's specification.
- **[System integrators](/en/sectors/system-integrators-custom-solutions):** belts that link machines and process steps.
- **[Recycling](/en/sectors/recycling)** and **[waste treatment](/en/sectors/waste-treatment):** belts that tolerate heat, sharp edges and heavy material.
- **[Water treatment](/en/sectors/water-treatment-filtration):** belts that scoop, extract or filter solids and sludge.
- **[Automotive](/en/sectors/automotive)**, **[solar and electronics](/en/sectors/solar-energy-electronics-)** and **[pharma and medical devices](/en/sectors/pharmacy-medical-devices):** precise transport through heat, chemicals and cleanroom-like conditions.
- **[Internal transport](/en/sectors/internal-transport-solutions):** robust belts for moving goods inside a plant.
