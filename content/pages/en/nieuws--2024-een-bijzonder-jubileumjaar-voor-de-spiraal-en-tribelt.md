## A milestone worth celebrating

For spring maker De Spiraal and its sister company Tribelt, the metal conveyor belt manufacturer in Haaksbergen, 2024 was a special year. Together the two companies look back on a combined history of more than 100 years, and they marked that milestone with pride.

Instead of the usual staff weekend away, the anniversary was celebrated with a programme of events spread over the whole year. Highlights ranged from a helicopter flight to a lively evening party, moments that brought colleagues together and gave them plenty to enjoy.

## Looking back and ahead

The year gave everyone a chance to reflect on where the companies came from and where they are heading. De Spiraal and Tribelt credit their employees for that success, and 2024 was the occasion to celebrate it together. The full company history is on the [about Tribelt](/en/about-us) page.
