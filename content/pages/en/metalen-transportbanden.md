## Six product families

Tribelt has developed and manufactured metal conveyor belts for many years. With its own engineering team and its own drive workshop in Haaksbergen, the Netherlands, it can take a project from first sketch through installation and maintenance. The range is split into six families.

### Eyelink belts

Metal belts made of wire eyes joined by crossrods and links, forming a flat and stable carrying surface. Five variants (OGB-L, OGB, OGB-LF, OGB-F and OGB-S) cover everything from bakery ovens to pharmaceutical sterilisers. [Eyelink belt details](/en/conveyor-belts/eye-link-belts).

### Open spiral belts (balanced weave)

Woven from alternating left- and right-hand wire spirals with an open mesh, so air, heat and liquid pass through freely. Ideal for ovens, cooling, drying and heat treatment up to about 1,150 °C. [Open spiral belt details](/en/conveyor-belts/balanced-weave-belts).

### Closed spiral belts (compound balanced)

A densely nested weave that gives an almost closed surface for very small products and even heat transfer, in widths up to 4,000 mm. [Closed spiral belt details](/en/conveyor-belts/compound-balanced-belts).

### Tri-Flex

A stainless steel belt of hinged links that runs straight or turns through curves, built for spiral freezers, spiral coolers, pasteurisers and smokers. [Tri-Flex details](/en/conveyor-belts/tri-flex).

### Drive elements

Sprockets, tube drums and cage drums machined in-house to fit each belt, for slip-free running and quick delivery. [Drive element details](/en/conveyor-belts/drive-elements).

### Other belts

Chain, ladder, plate link, honeycomb, interlaced wire and woven baking belts (Types 600 to 1700) for anything from light food handling to heavy, hot industrial processes. [Other belt types](/en/conveyor-belts/other-belts).
