## A key group of machine builders

Companies that build bakery equipment and industrial ovens are an important customer group for Tribelt, the metal conveyor belt manufacturer from Haaksbergen, the Netherlands. In an industrial bakery, belts carry dough, bread and pastry through ovens, coolers and packing lines around the clock. They must survive intense heat and constant load while remaining hygienic and easy to service.

## Built to the oven builder's specification

Tribelt makes belts that follow the oven builder's technical specification exactly. Ovens keep getting bigger and more efficient, and that raises demand for belts that scale with them, cope with heavy use and meet strict food safety rules. Tribelt's answer is custom belts in the right width, mesh and material, which has made it a supplier to oven builders around the world.

## What the market asks for

- **Heat resistance:** belts that keep their shape at baking temperatures for years.
- **Durability under load:** long runs with heavy product loads and frequent start-stop cycles.
- **Hygiene and maintenance:** open structures that are easy to clean and quick to service.
- **Scalability:** widths and lengths that grow with larger oven designs.
- **Compliance:** belts that satisfy food safety requirements in every market the oven is sold into.
