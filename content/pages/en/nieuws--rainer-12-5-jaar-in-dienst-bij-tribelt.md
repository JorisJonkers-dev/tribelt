## A celebration in the canteen

Rainer received a festive welcome in the Tribelt canteen, where colleagues looked back with him on more than twelve years of working together, growing the business and sharing good memories. He was surprised with a gift from the company and a personal present from his closest colleagues.

## Three decades of belt expertise

Rainer has more than 30 years of experience with metal conveyor belts, which makes him a true specialist. Tribelt is proud that he has been part of the company for so long and that his knowledge and involvement make a difference every day. Congratulations from all colleagues on this milestone.

Rainer Naumann is listed as Sales Germany in the [Tribelt team](/en/about-us).
