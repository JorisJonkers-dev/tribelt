## The backbone of material flow

Internal transport is the core of an efficient flow of goods. Companies depend on conveyors to move products through the plant safely, under control and with minimal stoppages. Tribelt, a Dutch manufacturer of metal conveyor belts, supplies robust, long-lasting belts both to builders of logistics installations and to the companies that use them.

Tribelt designs belts that slot into existing systems, so products travel quickly and reliably from storage to assembly and packing. The goal is a smoother internal flow, fewer stoppages and higher productivity.

## Receiving and intake

Internal transport starts where goods arrive. Speed, reliability and accuracy matter most here. Tribelt metal belts form a solid base for moving pallets, crates or bulk material from the loading docks to storage or production. Depending on the goods, belts can have anti-slip profiles, be made of stainless steel or have a reinforced build for heavy loads. Width, length and drive are agreed with the customer so the belt connects neatly to existing storage and handling systems.
