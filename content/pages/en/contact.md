## Contact details

Tribelt B.V. designs and manufactures metal conveyor belts and drives in Haaksbergen, in the east of the Netherlands. For questions, technical advice or a quotation, contact Tribelt directly:

- **Address:** Metaalstraat 9, 7483 PD Haaksbergen, the Netherlands
- **Phone:** +31 74 250 18 77
- **Email:** info@tribelt.nl

## Sending a request

This site has no forms. To send a message or a quote request, with attachments such as drawings or photos, use the contact form on Tribelt's official website via the button on this page. Tribelt aims to reply within 48 hours.

Useful details to include are the belt type or application, dimensions, belt speed, operating temperature and the product being conveyed. A sketch or photo is often enough to get started; see the [FAQ](/en/faq) for more.

## Visiting

Tribelt's factory and office in Haaksbergen share one site with sister company De Spiraal. A route planner is available on the official contact page.
