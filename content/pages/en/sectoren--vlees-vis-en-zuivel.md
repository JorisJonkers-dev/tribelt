## Hygiene first

Meat, fish and dairy processing leaves no room for compromise on hygiene, food safety or reliability. That is why the sector relies on stainless steel conveyors and belts designed to be cleaned easily and to carry heavy loads. Tribelt, a metal conveyor belt maker from Haaksbergen in the Netherlands, knows the belt types and the demands of each process.

Tribelt advises on the right material, belt construction and maintenance-friendly details, whether the belt must survive high heat or carry wet and sticky products. The aim is a line that runs continuously, safely and hygienically with as little downtime as possible.

## Meat

Metal belts are used throughout further processing: blanching, steaming, cooking, grilling, baking, frying, cooling and freezing. Tribelt supplies straight conveyors, curved belts and spiral towers, which allow a compact layout with high capacity. Flights, product carriers or side plates keep irregular or delicate products stable on the belt.

## Fish

Fish processing is tough on equipment. At sea and on land, belts must withstand moisture, salt and heavy mechanical loads. Tribelt makes these belts from high-grade stainless steel. On fishing vessels they are used to process, sort and move the catch, even in rough weather. In processing plants ashore they support glazing, freezing, chilling and packing, and the open mesh lets cold air reach every product evenly.

## Dairy and ready meals

Cheese, yoghurt ingredients and ready meals containing dairy are often chilled or frozen to extend shelf life. Tribelt belts fit tunnel and spiral tower installations and can be customised with side plates, flights or special widths. They stay stable and hygienic even in high humidity or deep cold.
