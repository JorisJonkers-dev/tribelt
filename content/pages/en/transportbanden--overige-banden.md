## A wider family of metal conveyor belts

Not every process fits an eyelink or spiral belt. Tribelt, the metal conveyor belt manufacturer from Haaksbergen in the Netherlands, therefore makes a second range of specialised belts, numbered by type. They cover light, hygienic food handling as well as heavy, hot and precision work: flexible corrugated mesh that lets air and water through, sturdy bar and chain belts for heavy logistics and straight tunnels, curved and spiral-capable belts for complex routes, and light woven belts for food.

## The types at a glance

| Type | Name | Key facts |
|---|---|---|
| 600 (WHG) | Conventional weave belt | Only left- or only right-hand spirals with welded ends; mesh 3–30 mm, wire 0.7–4 mm, widths up to 6,500 mm |
| 800 (STA) | Ladder belt | Modular bars; steel or stainless; very robust, accurate tracking, low maintenance |
| 1000 (KE) | Chain belt | Paired pre-stretched chains; sprocket drive without slip; wide choice of openings |
| 1200 (SCH) | Plate link belt | Slip-free drive, straight run, high load capacity, heat resistant |
| 1400 / 1500 (STRU/STRG) | Honeycomb belt | Slotted or straight strips; 1400 runs through curves, 1500 runs dead straight; widths 50–3,500 mm, cryogenic to about 600 °C |
| 1600 (GR) | Interlaced wire belt | Open, flexible mesh 2–32 mm, wire 0.9–2.8 mm, widths up to 6,000 mm, ambient to about 400 °C; single or double spirals, straight or crimped wire |
| 1700 (S) | Woven mesh baking belt | Very open, light, stretches little and holds its dimensions; low heat carry-over |

## Typical uses

- **Light, hygienic food work:** steaming, cooking, baking, frying, grilling, enrobing and sprinkling
- **Heavy industry:** logistics, containers, pallets, bulk goods, metallurgy, furnace transport and heat treatment
- **Special processes:** spiral and curved conveyors, tunnels for baking, freezing, pasteurising and smoking
- **Architecture and design:** ventilation grilles, façade cladding and decorative elements

## Options per type

- **Type 600:** choice of mesh and width, left- or right-hand spirals, single or double spirals
- **Type 800:** different bar profiles, steel, stainless or plastic, wear-resistant coatings
- **Type 1000:** various chains and materials, custom width and coupling, flights and side plates
- **Type 1200:** special slats to hold products, segmented build for very wide machines, many mesh/pitch combinations
- **Type 1500:** different locking methods, strip heights, thicknesses, widths and materials
- **Type 1600:** edge finishing on one or both sides, adjustable pitch and wire, flights and side chains
- **Type 1700:** mesh, wire type and material, brazed or beaded edges, reinforcing wires

## How they are driven

- **Friction:** corrugated mesh, light grille and woven belts over large drive rollers
- **Sprocket or chain drive:** chain belts, straight strip belts, curved belts and grille belts that need exact positioning
- **Positive or special drums:** plate link belts (Type 1200), with herringbone support where extra stability is needed
- **Combinations:** for spiral towers and curved tracks, from cryogenic to high-temperature processes
