## Small margins, high stakes

Making solar panels and semiconductors requires tightly controlled processes. Materials are fired in furnaces, given thin coatings or treated at high temperatures, and a small deviation can mean a defect or lost quality. Continuous measurement and control at every step, from coating to firing, catch problems early, protect product quality and prevent costly mistakes. Tribelt, the metal conveyor belt manufacturer from Haaksbergen, the Netherlands, supplies belts that carry products through these steps precisely.

## Processes Tribelt supports

- **Moving materials and components:** production starts with accurate handling of raw materials and semi-finished goods such as silicon wafers, glass sheets and electronic parts. Tribelt offers different widths, heights and materials depending on weight and fragility, and agrees belt direction, speed and surface finish with the customer to prevent breakage.
- **Assembly and processing:** when solar panels or electronic modules are assembled, each part must be in exactly the right position. Tribelt belts integrate with robots, welding and soldering stations and automatic inspection. Antistatic coatings, cleanable grids and adjustable frames help meet strict quality and safety requirements.
- **Quality control and efficiency:** belts can carry sensors, scanners and vision systems for real-time inspection so defects are found early. Modular sections and quickly adjustable lengths make product changeovers faster, raising throughput and consistency.
