## Meeting the baking industry

From the opening day to the last, the Tribelt team enjoyed meeting bakery professionals from all over the world at IBA 2025, the international trade fair for bakery and confectionery. Visitors' questions, feedback and enthusiasm made the fair a valuable experience.

Tribelt used the event to show its focus on high-quality metal conveyor belts, all made in its own factory in Haaksbergen, the Netherlands. The conversations on the stand confirmed the importance of three core values:

- **Quality**
- **Flexibility**
- **Responsiveness**

## Thanks and follow-up

Tribelt thanks everyone who visited, shared ideas and explored new possibilities, and looks forward to continuing those conversations after the fair. Anyone who could not make it to IBA is welcome to get in touch; bakery-specific belts are described on the [bread and pastry](/en/sectors/bread-pastry) page.
