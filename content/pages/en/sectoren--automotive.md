## Heat, chemicals and precision

Car parts go through coating and paint lines, heat treatment furnaces and industrial washers, all of which expose them to high temperatures, chemicals and intensive processing. The belts that carry them must be exceptionally strong, durable and reliable. Tribelt, a Dutch manufacturer of metal conveyor belts, supplies belts for these conditions so production keeps running, parts stay undamaged and vehicle quality is protected.

## Processes Tribelt supports

- **Assembly lines:** the heart of automotive production. Tribelt belts keep parts moving continuously, from small engine components to complete chassis. Modular stainless steel belts or belt systems with built-in stops are tailored to the customer's product variants and layout, after speed, weight capacity and flexibility have been worked out together.
- **Heavy components:** engine blocks, gearboxes and body parts call for precise and safe handling. Tribelt designs belts with high load capacity and robust construction, optionally with reinforced rollers, sprocket drives and slip-free surfaces. Width, profile and drive power are set for the weight and size of the parts to cut downtime.
- **Internal logistics:** efficient transport between warehouse, production and assembly determines overall throughput. Belts can run straight, angled or inclined, including level changes and bends, so parts move without extra handling. Vibration damping and special coatings protect sensitive parts.
