## Belts that clean water

In water treatment, solids, sludge and dirt must be removed without stopping the flow. Belts made by Tribelt, the Dutch metal conveyor belt manufacturer, work here as scoops, extractors or filters that take contamination out and leave clean water behind.

These conditions require sturdy, dependable belts. Tribelt develops belts specifically for water treatment, with durable materials and a design that keeps sludge and solids moving out continuously. That keeps maintenance and downtime low while water quality stays high.

## Processes Tribelt supports

- **Filtration:** material must be fed evenly and in a controlled way. Belts can be supplied with adjustable inclines and special perforations to drain water well and avoid clogging. For chemical or sludge-heavy use, Tribelt offers wear-resistant coatings and heat-resistant parts, and fits the belt to the existing filter installation and its hygiene and safety standards.
- **Sludge handling:** filtration produces sludge that has to be moved and processed reliably. Modular metal belts carry it to thickeners, dryers or presses. Optional cleaning systems and drainage grids keep the belt clean and working, and extra width or higher load capacity can be specified to match the plant.
- **Special designs:** stainless steel frames for corrosive surroundings, modular segments for quick replacement and sensors that monitor speed and load. Tribelt's engineers translate capacity, cleanability, safety and energy targets into a durable solution.
