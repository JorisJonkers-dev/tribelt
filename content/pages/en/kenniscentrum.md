## Why a knowledge centre

Tribelt, the Dutch maker of metal conveyor belts, does not only sell belts; it also shares what it knows about them. The knowledge centre collects practical explanations, tips and news about developments in conveyor belt technology. The articles are aimed at engineers, purchasers and anyone else who wants to understand which belt suits which process.

## Articles

- **[Tri-Flex: production, application and expertise](/en/knowledge-centre/tri-flex-production-application-and-expertise)**: how the hinged-link Tri-Flex belt handles curves in spiral freezers and coolers, with its key specifications.
- **[Open spiral belts: production, application and expertise](/en/knowledge-centre/open-spiral-belts-production-application-and-expertise)**: why an open woven spiral (balanced weave) belt suits ovens, dryers and sterilisers.
- **[Closed spiral belts: production, application and expertise](/en/knowledge-centre/closed-spiral-belts-production-application-and-expertise)**: how a dense compound balanced weave protects small products and spreads heat evenly.
- **[Eyelink belts: production, application and expertise](/en/knowledge-centre/wire-ring-belts-production-application-and-expertise)**: the construction, benefits and variants of eyelink belts.
