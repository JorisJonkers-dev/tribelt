## What a closed spiral belt is

A closed spiral belt is a metal conveyor belt woven so densely that its surface is nearly closed. In Tribelt's English product range it is listed as a **compound balanced belt**: several wire spirals are nested into each other and held by crossrods, with left- and right-hand spirals alternating so the belt runs straight. Tribelt makes every closed spiral belt in its own factory in Haaksbergen, the Netherlands.

The dense weave gives three things an open belt cannot: small parts stay on the belt, heat passes evenly into the product, and the belt stays stable under mechanical load. Tribelt tailors each belt to the process, whether the challenge is extreme heat, tiny products or unusual forces.

## Specifications

- **Pitch:** 5.0 to 25 mm
- **Mesh:** from 5.0 mm down to a fully closed surface, depending on wire thickness
- **Spiral wire:** 0.8 to 2.0 mm diameter (type-dependent)
- **Crossrod wire:** 1.0 to 3.15 mm diameter (type-dependent)
- **Wire strength:** all common grades, 500 to 1,050 N/mm²
- **Temperature:** ambient up to approximately 1,150 °C
- **Width:** up to 4,000 mm, depending on type
- **Build:** alternating left- and right-hand spirals, including the multi-insert wide spiral belt WG-I

## Applications

- **Bakery:** oven belts on which products are baked directly
- **Pharmaceuticals:** sterilisation tunnels
- **Metal and glass:** annealing, tempering, hardening, sintering and brazing
- **Machine building:** discharge belts under machining centres
- **Heat treatment:** curtains or screens that keep heat inside tunnel furnaces
- **Decorative work:** for example in jewellery

## Benefits

- Produced in-house, giving Tribelt full control of quality and lead time
- Dense surface that carries very small products
- Uniform heat transfer for evenly treated products
- Strong enough for high temperatures and heavy loads
- Straight, predictable running
- Suitable for both food and industrial processes

## Options

Side chains for a chain drive, flights or carriers, welded edges (on selected types), raised side plates for product guidance and PE1000 contact strips for quiet support.

## Drive

Closed spiral belts are friction-driven, which calls for a drive roller of sufficient diameter. Tribelt advises on the support structure as well; a herringbone layout with PE1000 wear strips is a common choice that keeps the belt stable and reduces wear. See [drive elements](/en/conveyor-belts/drive-elements) for rollers and sprockets, and the knowledge article on [closed spiral belts](/en/knowledge-centre/closed-spiral-belts-production-application-and-expertise) for more background.
