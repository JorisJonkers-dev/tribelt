## The belt as connector

System integrators combine, expand or adapt existing production systems to what an end customer needs. The conveyor belt is often the element that ties different machines and process steps together. Tribelt, a metal conveyor belt manufacturer in Haaksbergen, the Netherlands, works closely with these integrators.

That collaboration lets Tribelt deliver belts that fit each customer's production environment. Beyond linking machines, the right belt can improve material flow and make the whole system more efficient and reliable.

## Flexibility on every project

Each integration job is different and calls for a solution that fits the site. Tribelt supports integrators with a broad range of metal belts and the ability to adjust them: other dimensions, special materials, or versions with extra wear or heat resistance.

## Speed and delivery reliability

Integrators often work to short deadlines. Tribelt's own production capacity and direct communication let it switch quickly. Its engineers also think along about how the belt is used, so it performs well within the overall system design.
