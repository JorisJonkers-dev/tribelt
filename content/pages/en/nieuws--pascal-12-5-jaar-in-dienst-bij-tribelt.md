## An anniversary by choice

Pascal has now worked at Tribelt for twelve and a half years. What makes the milestone stand out is that he could have retired a year earlier. Out of love for the trade and commitment to the product, he decided to keep going, and the company is glad he did.

## The face for French-speaking customers

For years Pascal has been the regular contact for Tribelt's French-speaking customers. Colleagues and clients value his dedication, professionalism and warmth, and Tribelt used the occasion to thank him, in his own language, for his effort and commitment over the years. Congratulations from all colleagues.

Pascal Poirier is listed as Sales France in the [Tribelt team](/en/about-us).
