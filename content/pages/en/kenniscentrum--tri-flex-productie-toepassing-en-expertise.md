## Why flexibility matters

Modern production lines need conveyors that are flexible, precise and dependable. Tri-Flex, a stainless steel conveyor belt developed and made by Tribelt in Haaksbergen, the Netherlands, stands out because of its modular build, smooth running and the range of processes it fits. This article explains how it works and where it is used.

## How Tri-Flex is built

Tri-Flex is a metal belt made of hinged links. The hinges allow the belt to follow a straight conveyor and also to turn smoothly through bends. That means complex routes, in spiral towers or on straight lines, can be built without giving up reliability or accuracy.

Tribelt produces Tri-Flex entirely in its own factory, from design to finished belt, so material, dimensions and drive can be tailored to each installation. Details such as round recesses in the links for better contact, and oval eyes at the spiral ends for extra safety, show the engineering behind the belt.

### Key specifications

- **Pitch:** 19.05 mm, 27.4 mm or 38.1 mm
- **Width:** 270 to 1,400 mm in spiral towers; 270 to 2,000 mm on straight lines
- **Temperature:** from -50 °C up to 400 °C, depending on the application
- **Materials:** several stainless steel grades for durability and hygiene

## Benefits

- **Tight turns:** hinged links let the belt bend around small radii, ideal for spiral towers.
- **Wide use:** works on straight conveyors and in complex spiral systems, including cryogenic freezing.
- **Hygiene:** designed for food, with surfaces that are easy to clean and well-defined product contact zones.
- **Reliability:** precise production gives a consistent finish, long life and steady running.
- **Low maintenance:** the belt can be turned over for extra life.
- **Safety:** oval eyes and raised side links keep products on track, even at speed or under load.

## Applications

Tri-Flex is used worldwide wherever product temperature must be controlled precisely:

- **Food processing:** cooling, freezing, pasteurising, smoking and cooking.
- **Spiral towers:** cooling towers and spiral freezers, where a long belt path fits into a small floor area.
- **Straight lines:** for example liquid nitrogen shock freezers that need a stable, reliable run.

Its versatility also makes it useful in other sectors that need accurate guidance, temperature resistance and hygiene.

## Variants and custom options

- **Spiral overlays** with variable pitch and wire gauge for good product support
- **One-sided weaving** for small pitches (below 4.25 mm)
- **Raised side links** for guiding products
- **Stainless steel grades** chosen for temperature, hygiene and mechanical load
- **Support systems:** radial strips for spiral conveyors, herringbone for straight lines

Tribelt also designs the drive: purpose-made sprockets, careful tuning of the overdrive and positioning on idler shafts keep the belt gripping and running smoothly. Full data are on the [Tri-Flex product page](/en/conveyor-belts/tri-flex).

## Why choose Tri-Flex

A Tri-Flex belt is a critical link in the production chain. Tribelt combines good materials, careful design and precise manufacturing so the belt performs in extreme conditions. For operators that means higher efficiency, less downtime, consistent product quality and a long service life. For companies looking for flexibility, hygiene and durability in their conveying, Tri-Flex is a strong option.
