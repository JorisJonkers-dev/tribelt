## Belts for fourteen sectors

Tribelt makes custom metal conveyor belts and drives for a broad mix of industries, from bread and pastry to fruit, vegetable and meat processing. The common thread is a production line that must keep running reliably and efficiently. Pick a sector to see which processes Tribelt belts support there and which belt types are used most.

### Food

- [Bread and pastry](/en/sectors/bread-pastry): ovens, cooling and freezing, proofing and dough handling
- [Potatoes, fruit and vegetables](/en/sectors/potato-fruit-and-vegetables): washing, blanching, frying, oil filtering, drying and freezing
- [Meat, fish and dairy](/en/sectors/meat-fish-and-dairy): cooking, grilling, glazing, chilling and freezing
- [Meat substitutes](/en/sectors/meat-substitutes): raw material handling, extrusion, forming, coating and packing

### Machine and line builders

- [Bakery and oven builders](/en/sectors/bakery-and-oven-builders): heat-resistant belts built to the oven builder's specification
- [Production lines and process installations](/en/sectors/production-lines-process-installations): belts for complete lines in food, industry and automotive
- [System integrators and custom solutions](/en/sectors/system-integrators-custom-solutions): belts that connect machines and process steps

### Industry and environment

- [Recycling](/en/sectors/recycling): glass, plastic and metal that are hot, sharp or heavy
- [Waste treatment](/en/sectors/waste-treatment): sorting, processing and removing residual waste
- [Water treatment and filtration](/en/sectors/water-treatment-filtration): removing solids and sludge from water
- [Automotive](/en/sectors/automotive): coating, painting, heat treatment and assembly
- [Solar energy and electronics](/en/sectors/solar-energy-electronics-): precise handling of wafers, glass and components
- [Pharmacy and medical devices](/en/sectors/pharmacy-medical-devices): sterilisation and hygienic handling of vials and devices
- [Internal transport](/en/sectors/internal-transport-solutions): moving goods safely through a plant
